About Us

A professional legacy built on trust, service and assurance.

Four reputed Chartered Accountancy firms, ten senior public accountants and more than four decades of practice — consolidated into one firm in 2012.

ARTISAN Chartered Accountants is a merger of 4 (four) reputed Accounting and Consulting firms with Ten (10) Senior Public Accountants hitherto practicing about more than 40 (Forty) years, however, 4 (Four) Partners retired including 1 (one) died. Currently Six (6) number of partners practices in the name of ARTISAN Chartered Accountants.

ARTISAN has been providing a comprehensive range of business services in the areas ranging from simple accounting to IFRS implementation, outsourcing, internal auditing, external and statutory audit performance and assurance, IPO, annual report, CG compliance certification, taxation and a wide range of business advisory and consultancy services.

The firm completed and engaged various natures of works under Govt. Agencies Units of Sector Corporation (Industries & Commerce), NGO’s, Public Companies, Autonomous Bodies, Multi-national Companies, Banks, Insurance, Utility Services (Electricity, Gas, Water, etc.) with their long practicing life as public accountants.
