Terms of Reference

Scope, responsibilities and professional principles governing every ARTISAN engagement.

1. Purpose

The purpose of this Terms of Reference (ToR) is to define the scope, responsibilities, and professional principles under which ARTISAN Chartered Accountants delivers its services. This document establishes the framework for maintaining quality, integrity, independence, and compliance across all professional engagements.

2. About ARTISAN Chartered Accountants

ARTISAN Chartered Accountants is a professional accounting and consulting firm established through the merger of four reputable Chartered Accountancy firms. Backed by over four decades of combined professional experience and led by six practicing partners, the firm delivers reliable accounting, assurance, taxation, and business advisory services to a diverse range of clients.

3. Scope of Professional Services

ARTISAN Chartered Accountants provides a comprehensive range of professional services, including but not limited to:

  • Accounting and Bookkeeping Services
  • Audit and Assurance Services
  • Statutory and External Audits
  • Internal Audit
  • IFRS Implementation
  • Outsourced Accounting Solutions
  • Tax Planning and Compliance
  • Corporate Governance Compliance Certification
  • IPO Advisory and Financial Reporting
  • Annual Report Preparation
  • Business Advisory and Consulting Services
  • Financial Reporting and Regulatory Compliance

4. Professional Responsibilities

The firm is committed to:

  • Delivering professional services with competence and due care.
  • Maintaining independence, objectivity, and ethical standards.
  • Protecting the confidentiality of client information.
  • Complying with applicable laws, regulations, and professional standards.
  • Providing practical, accurate, and value-driven business solutions.
  • Maintaining transparent communication throughout each engagement.

5. Client Responsibilities

Clients are expected to:

  • Provide complete and accurate information required for professional engagements.
  • Cooperate throughout the assignment by supplying requested documentation.
  • Review reports and recommendations within agreed timelines.
  • Fulfil agreed contractual and financial obligations.

6. Quality Assurance

Every engagement is performed in accordance with recognized professional standards and internal quality control procedures. ARTISAN Chartered Accountants is committed to continuous improvement, technical excellence, and delivering services that meet the highest standards of professional practice.

7. Confidentiality

All client information obtained during professional engagements will be treated as confidential unless disclosure is required by applicable law, professional regulations, or with the client’s written consent.

8. Independence and Ethics

ARTISAN Chartered Accountants performs its services with integrity, independence, objectivity, and professional competence. Every engagement is conducted in accordance with the ethical principles expected of Chartered Accountants.

9. Review and Updates

This Terms of Reference may be reviewed and updated periodically to reflect changes in professional standards, regulatory requirements, or the firm’s operational practices.
