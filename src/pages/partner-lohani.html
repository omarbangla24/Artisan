Mr. Md. Abdul Momin Khan Lohani is a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Bangladesh since 1989; qualified as Chartered Accountant in the year 1989 and his membership no. under ICAB being 575. He had spent almost eighteen years in various organizations as senior financial management specialist, which inculcated in him the diversified experience of different aspects of running the business.

During his eighteen years of industry based experience, he has gained vast knowledge in the fields of accountancy, financial & general management, taxation, risk management, human resource management and information system development with the prerogative to help organizations and individuals to achieve their objectives. He was formal Board Of Director of Mercantile Bank Ltd. He has more than eighteen years’ experience in public practice.
