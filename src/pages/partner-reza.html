Mr. Md. Selim Reza has obtained numerous qualifications pertaining to the field of finance and being a well-known fellow member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Bangladesh, and a distinguished member of the Institute of the Institute of Chartered Secretaries of Bangladesh (ICSB); he qualified as a member of the ICAB in the year 2006 and his membership no. under ICAB being 939. He is also a member of the Canadian Association of Business Administration (CABA) and the Institute of Personnel Management (IPM).

Before joining into practice, he worked in a Managerial position, as a Manager Finance, Manager Cost, Financial Controller and Company Secretary and worked closely with the objective of preparing organizational manuals, conducting basic and advanced level training and the implementation of software. In addition, he had conducted training for numerous other organizations in the fields of accounts, audit, and financial management.
